# LEGO BOOST Move Hub

LEGO BOOST Move Hub is a Bluetooth LE hub that drives up to four motors or other Powered Up / BOOST peripherals.

![Boost](../../../../BrickController2/BrickController2/UI/Images/boost_image.png)

## Channels

The device exposes 4 channels:

| Channels | Purpose | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 - 4 | Ports A, B, C, D | -100 % to +100 % |

Ports A and B are the hub's built-in motor ports.

## Getting started

1. Power on the hub by pressing its button.
2. Open the device list and press **Scan for new devices**. Found devices are added to the list.
3. Use the device in a creation: assign its channels to controller actions.

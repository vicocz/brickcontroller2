# LEGO DUPLO Train Hub

LEGO DUPLO Train Hub is a Bluetooth LE hub built into the DUPLO train. It drives the train motor.

![DUPLO Train Hub](../../../../BrickController2/BrickController2/UI/Images/duplotrainhub_image.png)

## Channels

The device exposes 1 channel:

| Channels | Purpose | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Train motor - A | -100 % to +100 % |

## Getting started

1. Power on the train hub by pressing its button.
2. Open the device list and press **Scan for new devices**. Found devices are added to the list.
3. Use the device in a creation: assign its channel to a controller action.
